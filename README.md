# powerbi_transaction_analysis

## 📊 Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of customer, store, and product performance across different regions and categories. It enables data-driven insights into sales quantity, customer distribution, and store activity across countries and states.

## 🚀 Key Insights

Total Quantity Sold: 198K units

Customer Distribution: Highest number of customers from California and Texas

Global Reach: Active customers and stores across United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Canada, and Australia

Top Store Locations:

🇺🇸 United States – 42.36%

🇬🇧 United Kingdom – 21.26%

🇩🇪 Germany – 10.58%

Top Product Categories by Quantity:

### 💻 Computers – 22.33%

### 📱 Cell Phones – 15.92%

### 🎧 Audio – 11.88%

### 📺 TV and Video – 8.9%

### 🌍 Dashboard Features

Interactive filters for Customer State and Customer City

Dynamic visuals including bar charts, line graphs, pie charts, and maps

Real-time geo-analysis showing customer concentration by country

Product-wise and country-wise sales performance tracking

## 🧩 Visual Components

Count of CustomerID by State – Bar chart showing top states by customer count

Sum of Quantity by Country – Line graph comparing total sold quantities per country

Count of StoreID by Country – Pie chart illustrating store distribution

Sum of Quantity by Product Category – Donut chart displaying category-wise sales share

Customer Country Map – Geographic map showing customer density

## 💡 Purpose

Designed to demonstrate data visualization and business intelligence skills using Power BI. This dashboard helps organizations track customer engagement, product demand, and regional performance to support strategic decision-making.

## 🛠 Tools Used

Microsoft Power BI

Excel / CSV Dataset (for data import)

DAX & Power Query (for calculations and data transformation)

## 🏁 Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard offers a detailed, interactive, and data-driven view of organizational sales activity. It assists in identifying high-performing regions, customer segments, and product categories, which can guide business decision-making and strategy formulation.
